Delete Your Account

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please read this page carefully before proceeding.

⚠️

This action is permanent

Once you delete your account, all your data is immediately removed and cannot be recovered. There is no undo.

📱 How to Delete Your Account

Follow these steps inside the Barks and Meows app:

1
Open the Barks and Meows app on your device
2
Navigate to the Profile tab in the bottom navigation
3
Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
4
Read the confirmation message, then tap "Delete" to confirm

🗑️ What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

👤

Account Info

Your email address and authentication credentials

🐾

Pet Profiles

All pet names, ages, and categories you created

💉

Vaccine Records

All vaccination notes, dates, and doctor information

⚙️

App Preferences

Any settings or preferences you configured

🕐 Data Retention

Immediate deletion: All your data is deleted immediately upon account deletion.

Backup copies in our systems may take up to 30 days to be fully purged. Some data may be retained as required by law for fraud prevention purposes.

💡 Before You Delete

  • This is irreversible — you cannot recover your account or data after deletion
  • Save your data first — we don't currently offer data export, so save important information manually
  • Just need a break? — You can log out and come back anytime without losing your data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 4, 2026

Summary

Peyar does not collect any personal information. All name generation and favourites are stored locally on your device. We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data to improve the app.

📋 Introduction

Peyar ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Tamil name generator handles information. The app is designed with a privacy-first approach.

📥 Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Favourite Names: Names you mark as favourites are stored locally using Android DataStore
  • App Preferences: Basic settings and preferences are stored locally

All locally stored data stays exclusively on your device. We have no access to it.

Anonymous Usage Data

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data such as feature interactions and session information. This data contains no personally identifiable information and is used solely to understand how the app is used and improve it over time.

🚫 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Access to contacts, photos, or other personal files
  • Account creation or login is not required
  • Personal data is never shared with third parties

🤖 On-Device AI Model

The app includes an embedded TensorFlow Lite model that runs entirely on your device. It does not send any data to external servers and generates Tamil names locally without requiring an internet connection.

💾 Data Storage & Security

  • All personal data is stored locally using Android's secure DataStore
  • Your favourites and preferences are accessible only by the app
  • All locally stored data is deleted when you uninstall the app

⚖️ Your Rights

  • Favourites: Add or remove favourite names at any time within the app
  • App data: Clear all app data through Android settings at any time
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all locally stored data

✅ Compliance

GDPR CCPA COPPA Google Play

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.

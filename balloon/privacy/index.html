Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 6, 2026

Summary

Balloon does not collect personal information. Only your high score is stored locally on your device. We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data to improve the game. No account is required.

📋 Introduction

Balloon ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Balloon mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

📥 Information We Collect

Locally Stored Data

High Score: Your best game score is saved locally on your device using Android DataStore. This data is never transmitted to any server or third party and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Anonymous Usage Data

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data such as session information and feature interactions. This data contains no personally identifiable information and is used to understand how the game is played and to improve the experience over time.

🚫 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Access to contacts, camera, or microphone
  • No account creation or login required

🔒 Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal data, there is no risk of personal data breach from our end. All data remains on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
